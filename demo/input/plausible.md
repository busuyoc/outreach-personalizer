## Cached snapshot (source: https://plausible.io/vs-google-analytics, retrieved 2026-08-28)

Data accuracy: GA4's "consent mode" fills gaps with behavioral modeling when
visitors decline cookies, blending real and estimated data. Plausible
collects no personal data and needs no consent banner — only actual visits.

Usability: GA4 needs Tag Manager setup, custom events, and "Explorations"
for basic tasks that were simpler in Universal Analytics. Plausible puts all
key metrics on one dashboard, no configuration.

Performance: GA4's tracking script is 135KB gzipped; Plausible's is 2.5KB
(54x smaller).

Privacy & legal: data protection authorities in seven EU nations ruled GA4
violates GDPR over data transfers. Plausible processes all data on EU
servers, no cookie consent banner required.

Ad blockers: an independent study found GA4 fails to capture an average of
55.6% of traffic vs. cookieless analytics. Plausible is far less likely to
be blocked since it isn't part of the adtech ecosystem.

Transparency: GA4's source is not auditable; Plausible's is open source on
GitHub.

Business model: GA4 is free because Google monetizes behavioral data for
ads. Plausible charges subscriptions specifically to stay independent of
that model.
